## Background and Formula 1 debut

Fernando Alonso is a Spanish racing driver born in Oviedo,
Spain, on 29 July 1981.

Alonso made his Formula 1 race debut with Minardi in 2001.
He became a Renault test driver in 2002 and joined Renault's
race lineup in 2003.

## First Formula 1 victory

Alonso won his first Formula 1 Grand Prix at the 2003
Hungarian Grand Prix while driving for Renault.

## World championships

Alonso won the Formula 1 Drivers' Championship with Renault
in 2005 and again in 2006.

In 2005, he became the youngest Drivers' Champion at that
time, aged 24. In 2006, he successfully defended his
championship against Michael Schumacher.

## Later career milestones

Alonso raced alongside Lewis Hamilton at McLaren in 2007.
He subsequently returned to Renault and joined Ferrari in 2010.

After two seasons away from Formula 1, Alonso returned
to the championship with Alpine in 2021.
