# Max Verstappen

Source: https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers/max-verstappen
Checked on: 2026-09-09
Coverage: Selected historical facts through the 2024 season.

## Background

Max Verstappen is a Dutch Formula 1 racing driver. He raced
for Toro Rosso before moving to Red Bull Racing.

## First Formula 1 victory

Verstappen won the 2016 Spanish Grand Prix in Barcelona
on his first race appearance for Red Bull Racing.

He was 18 years and 228 days old, making him the youngest
Grand Prix winner in Formula 1 history at that time.

## First world championship

Verstappen won his first Formula 1 Drivers' Championship
in 2021. The championship contest with Lewis Hamilton was
decided at the final race of the season in Abu Dhabi.

## Further championships

Verstappen won another Drivers' Championship in 2022,
followed by championships in 2023 and 2024.

By the end of the 2024 season, Verstappen had won four
consecutive Drivers' Championships, all with Red Bull Racing.
