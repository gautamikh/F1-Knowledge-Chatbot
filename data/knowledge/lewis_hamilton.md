## Background and early career

Lewis Hamilton is a British racing driver born in Stevenage,
England, on 7 January 1985. He competed in karting before
progressing to single-seater racing. Hamilton won the GP2
championship in 2006.

## Formula 1 debut

Hamilton began racing in Formula 1 with McLaren in 2007.
His teammate that season was Fernando Alonso. Hamilton won
four races in his debut season and finished one point behind
champion Kimi Raikkonen.

## World championships

Hamilton won his first Formula 1 Drivers' Championship with
McLaren in 2008.

He joined Mercedes in 2013. With Mercedes, Hamilton won the
Drivers' Championship in 2014, 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019, and 2020.
By the end of 2020, he had won seven Drivers' Championships.

## First Mercedes victory

Hamilton's first Grand Prix victory for Mercedes came at
the 2013 Hungarian Grand Prix.
